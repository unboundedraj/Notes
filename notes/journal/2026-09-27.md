This week brought meaningful progress across several fronts. I'm grateful for the wins and the lessons learned from the inevitable challenges.

## Wins from This Week

- Shipped the first version of the Cortex app—exciting to see months of planning come to life
- Made significant progress on the notes data model, which is now stable and ready for real users
- Had a productive meeting with the team about the roadmap for Q4
- Completed three books from my reading list

## Lessons Learned

- Perfectionism can be the enemy of progress; shipping an 80% solution was the right call
- Investing in clear documentation early saves countless hours of debugging later
- Asking for help earlier (rather than grinding alone) speeds up problem-solving
- Small, consistent habits compound much faster than I initially expected

## Looking Ahead

Next week I want to focus on user feedback and iterate on the core editing experience. The foundation is solid—now it's about making it delightful.
